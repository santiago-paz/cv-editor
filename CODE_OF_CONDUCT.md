# Code of conduct

This project is open to anyone who wants to help. Be kind and keep the talk on
the work.

## What the maintainer expects

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask when something is unclear.
- No harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- Keep private things private, yours and other people's. Do not post a real CV or
  personal data in an issue.

## What happens when someone breaks the rules

The maintainer may edit or delete a comment, close a thread or block a person.
This covers issues, pull requests, comments and commits.

## Report a problem

Write to the contact address on the privacy page: https://trycveditor.com/privacy

The maintainer treats every report as private.
